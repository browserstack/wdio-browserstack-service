- Supports WebdriverIO 10. This release requires WebdriverIO 10 and Node.js 22.19 or later. For WebdriverIO 9 projects, use the `v9` release line (`npm i -D @wdio/browserstack-service@v9`); for WebdriverIO 8, use `@wdio/browserstack-service@v8`.
- Accessibility Automation, including per-command auto-scans, works on WebdriverIO 10.
